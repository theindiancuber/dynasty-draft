      Your banners

      Every season you finish is kept here, in this browser.

      Each pack is a franchise's core for one decade, roughly the ten players with the most games. Card stats are per-game averages across only that player's seasons with that team in that decade, compiled by hand and approximate. Ratings and the sim are a fan model, not official data. Player photos load from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons and are free-licensed only; flip a card for its credit link.

      How it works

      1. Each pack is a team of the decade, say the 1990s Bulls. Tear it open to get 6 of their 10 core players. Stars are rare pulls, cool off right after you land one, and come due after a dry spell.
      2. Pick one player for your eight-man rotation. His card shows only that decade's numbers.
      3. Cards only fit the positions they played. Bench spots take a guard, a wing and a big.
      4. Don't like the draw? You can reroll once. Some packs carry luck: a Moments card (+3 OVR) or a bonus reroll.
      5. Watch your chemistry. Title teams have a closer and a second star, a floor general, shooters, two-way wings and a rim protector. Teammates from the same pack bring continuity. Too many players who need the ball, or old feuds like Warriors Durant and Westbrook, cost you.
      6. Once per draft, make a mystery trade: give up a player for a sealed 3-card pack at his spot. Each card has a 2% chance to be a 99.
      7. Flip a card for its matchup splits: how that player's game holds up against each legend's style.
      8. Your eight plays a 16-game gauntlet, home and away against eight all-time teams. Watch each game live with win odds, or sim ahead.
      9. The top four make the playoffs, where rotations tighten and starters play 40 minutes. Win two best-of-seven series to hang a banner.
