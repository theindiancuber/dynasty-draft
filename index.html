      How it works

      1. A random draw gives you a franchise and a decade, say Chicago Bulls 1990s.
      2. Pick one player from that pack for your eight-man rotation.
      3. Cards only fit the positions they played. Bench spots take a guard, a wing and a big.
      4. Don't like the draw? You can reroll once.
      5. Watch your chemistry: a floor general, shooters, a rim protector, and not too many ball-dominant scorers.
      6. Flip a card for its matchup splits: how that player's game holds up against each legend's style.
      7. Your eight plays a 16-game gauntlet, home and away against eight all-time teams.
      8. The top four make the playoffs. Win two best-of-seven series to hang a banner.
